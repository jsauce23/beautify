What is Teeth Whitening?

Beautified Studios offers in-studio teeth whitening using a professional LED whitening light. You relax in the chair while the whitening gel and light work together to lift surface stains, and you leave with a brighter smile the same day.

01

How it works

A whitening gel is applied to the teeth and activated with a professional LED light to brighten your smile.

02

Who it is for

Anyone with surface stains from coffee, tea, wine or everyday life who wants a brighter smile before an event or just because.

03

Your appointment

Lips and gums are protected, the gel is applied, and you relax under the light. Pair it with a tooth gem for extra sparkle.

Most popular combo

Whitening + a tooth gem
