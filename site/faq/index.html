Booking & payment

How do I book an appointment?+

Book online any time on our booking page or right on any treatment page. You can also call or text (726) 242-2952.

Do I need to pay when I book?+

You can hold your appointment with a card and pay at your visit, pay in full online, or choose buy now, pay later.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel. Cancellations with less notice are charged 50% of the scheduled service.

Do you offer financing?+

Yes. Buy now, pay later is available in studio for treatments from $50 to $4,000. Get approved before your appointment.

Treatments

What is the difference between a lip flip and lip filler?+

A lip flip relaxes the muscle above your lip so it rolls slightly outward for a subtle pout. Lip filler adds real volume and shape with RHA filler.

What is Daxxify?+

Daxxify is a wrinkle relaxing injectable, similar to Botox, known for lasting longer between appointments for many clients.

What is RHA filler?+

RHA is a hyaluronic acid filler designed to move with your facial expressions, so results look natural when you talk and smile.

Is a non-surgical nose job permanent?+

No. It uses filler, so results are temporary, with no surgery involved.

What is included in Full Facial Balancing?+

Three syringes of filler placed where your face needs them most plus a complimentary lip flip, for $1,954.

What is a Perfecting Session?+

A follow up visit to fine tune your Botox or filler results.
