What is Lip Blush?

Lip blush is a permanent makeup treatment that adds a soft wash of color to the lips. It can define the lip line, even out uneven tone and give you a natural tint that lasts, so you can skip daily lip liner and lipstick.

01

How it works

Pigment is placed into the lips to add soft color and definition, customized to your skin tone.

02

Who it is for

Clients with pale or uneven lip color, or anyone who wants defined lips without daily makeup.

03

Your appointment

A color consultation, numbing, then treatment. Color looks bold at first and softens as it heals.
