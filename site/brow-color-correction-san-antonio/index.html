What is Brow Correction?

Beautified Studios specializes in permanent makeup, including color correcting and all brows gone bad situations. If old brow tattoos or microblading have turned red, blue or gray, or healed uneven, color correction can neutralize the tone and restore a natural brow.

01

How it works

Corrective pigment is used to neutralize unwanted tones in old brow work, then the brow is refined to a natural shape and color.

02

Who it is for

Clients with old brow tattoos or microblading that has faded, changed color or healed uneven.

03

Your appointment

A consultation to assess your current brows, a color correction plan, then treatment. Some brows need more than one session.

Brows gone bad?

What color correction can fix

Red or pink brows

Old pigment that healed warm.

Blue or gray brows

Cool tones from aged tattoo ink.

Faded, patchy brows

Microblading that did not hold evenly.

Uneven shape

Brows that healed too thick, thin or lopsided.
