What is Nose Job?

Also called a liquid rhinoplasty, a non-surgical nose job uses filler to smooth bumps, straighten the look of the bridge and refine your profile. It is a precise treatment, and precision is what Brandy is known for.

01

How it works

Filler placed strategically on the nose to even out its shape and profile. It does not make the nose smaller.

02

Who it is for

Clients bothered by a bump, dip or uneven bridge who want a change without surgery.

03

Your appointment

A thorough consultation, numbing and careful placement. Plan for about 90 minutes.

Set the right expectations

What a non-surgical nose job can and cannot do

It can

  • ✓Smooth a bump on the bridge
  • ✓Straighten the look of the nose
  • ✓Lift a drooping tip
  • ✓Even out dips and asymmetry

It cannot

  • ×Make the nose smaller
  • ×Fix breathing problems
  • ×Give permanent results
  • ×Replace surgery in every case

Nose Job questions

Is a non-surgical nose job permanent?+

No. It uses filler, so results are temporary and there is no surgery or operating room recovery.

How much is a non-surgical nose job?+

It starts at $1,500, with the final price confirmed at your consultation.

Can it make my nose smaller?+

Filler adds volume, so it cannot reduce size. It can make the nose look straighter and more balanced.
